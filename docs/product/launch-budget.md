At the maximum $5,000 currently available, even the lower case has an estimated

launch commitments. The displayed dollars are calculated totals, not precision

The cases assume 25–100 invited creators, not 500 active creators on day one.
Founder labor supplies development and routine moderation/support without a
salary. The $100–$600 monthly paid backup allowance buys limited help, not
continuous staffing. Define the founder's available hours, review workload and
qualified backup before real activity; reduce admissions if capacity is smaller.

Processing rates, fixed fees, refunds, chargebacks, payouts/FX, member age checks,
verification repeats, media processing/moderation and excess usage remain
unquoted variable expenses. Processor deposits and rolling holds also remain
unknown: they restrict usable cash and are separate from ordinary expenses.
Taxes, insurance and any additional professional or regulatory requirements
could raise the target. Household expenses and existing AI subscriptions are
excluded. No revenue is assumed.

Spend and review in sequence:

1. Finish the business definition. No purchase or provider signup is needed.
2. Obtain one adult-platform counsel discovery quote with a cap the founder
   approves. The allowance is $300–$800; it is for initial advice, not all legal
   documents. Then obtain the necessary entity/tax advice and processor fit/terms.
3. Once the model and cash needs are understood, commission a scoped technical
   review before the money, ledger, entitlement and media designs become fixed.
   The unquoted allowance is $500–$1,500. Fiverr can be a source of candidates:
   require relevant experience, a written scope, concrete findings and remediation
   guidance. Marketplace ratings alone do not establish competence.
4. Before real users, identity documents, creator uploads or money, obtain the
   applicable counsel-approved documents, an independent integrated security
   assessment and retest, and operational coverage. Allowances are $2,000–$6,000
   for legal documents/consent and $1,500–$5,000 for technical assessment/retest;
   qualified quotes may exceed them. A cheap generic website review does not
   substitute for either engagement.

Choose transactional email later, after the business/entity, processor fit,
country/data boundaries and funded staging plan are known, and before external
registration, verification or password-reset flows. Keep the API's production
mail guard until approved delivery is configured and tested.

The founding creator direction remains approximately 500 creators, necessary
processing fees only and a founder badge. Eligibility, duration and fee coverage
still need definition. With zero platform commission those sales provide no
contribution toward operations. Plus income is unproven; top-100 memberships and
leaderboard rewards are provisional and excluded from this budget.
